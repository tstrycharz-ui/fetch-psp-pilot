# Best Friends Pet Care — Company Defaults

These policies apply to **all Best Friends locations** unless a brand or location file explicitly overrides them. When answering a question, if a location file is silent on a topic, use this file as the answer and disclose the source: *"I don't have a specific policy for [location] on this — the Best Friends standard is..."*

Never use the word "corporate" in any user-facing answer. Use "Best Friends standard" or "company default" instead.

## Boarding Policies

### What Owners Can Bring
- Pre-packaged pet food and treats
- Up to 2 toys
- **No outside bedding, towels, or clothing** (Best Friends provides all bedding and bowls)

> Location exception: Some locations allow personal bedding in specific room types. Check location file.

### Food
- Best Friends can provide both wet and dry food at **no additional cost**
- In-house diet: **Wellness Lamb**
- Maximum **3 meals per day** per pet

### Drop-Off
- Drop-off for boarding is anytime up to **30 minutes prior to closing**

### Medication Policy
- Pets with medical conditions or medications (illness, injury, incontinence, mobility issues) must speak to the center directly to confirm they can be accommodated — **cannot guarantee boarding**
- **We do not accept any pets that require injections**

### Shared Accommodations
- Each additional pet sharing a room: **20% off** (does not apply to pocket pets)
- Unaltered pets are **not allowed** to share accommodations or attend group play
- Pets that need to be fed separately are **not eligible** to share rooms (due to kennel setup and safety)

### Animals We Cannot Accept
- Primates
- Venomous animals

### Pigs
- Charged under dog boarding rates
- Ideally placed in an all-indoor or luxury room
- Cannot be charged as a small animal or pocket pet

### Parasites
- Pets with worms or parasites are **not accepted** for boarding, daycare, or camp
- Must have a **negative fecal exam** before attending

### Temperature Management
- Team members check temperature multiple times daily
- During hot summers and cold winters: activities are limited, patio doors are closed, pets and people are kept well hydrated and under-exerted

## Vaccination Policy

- Vaccinations **cannot be self-administered**
- Vaccine records **cannot be handwritten**
- Must be provided by a **licensed veterinarian**

## Grooming Policies

### Service Types
- **Bath:** Wash, dry, ear cleaning, and nail trimming
- **Haircut / Trim / Comfort Clip:** Includes bath
- Grooming costs are based on **breed, requested services, and coat condition**

| Aromatherapy | $31 | Lavender chamomile shampoo and conditioner, teeth brushing, facial |
| Itchy & Scratchy | $31 | Milk & Honey shampoo and conditioner, teeth brushing, nail grinding |
| Shedless | $41 / $51 / $61 | Sweet orange shed control shampoo and conditioner, loose undercoat removal, teeth brushing, nail grinding |

### Pickup Time
- We cannot give a specific pickup time for grooming — the groomer will call when the pet is ready
- Recommend reaching out directly to the facility for an estimate

### Sedated Grooming
- We **do not offer** sedated grooming
- Refer the customer to the center, which can refer them to an approved veterinarian

## Complaints and Concerns

For any complaints, concerns, or general comments about a recent experience:
- Direct the customer to provide their name, phone number, and a detailed description of what happened
- A team member from the correct department will reach out to them
- Do **not** attempt to resolve complaints on the CSS call — always route to the location
