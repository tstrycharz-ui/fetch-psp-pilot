Margin for error, and what we caught
  • Hours are estimates. Each task is logged as what it would have taken by hand. Direction time is measured from session history.
  • Caught before launch. Fetch's read-backs found every Lapsed run ending at day 1. All 371 checks were fixed on Oct 22, before the 77 locations went live.
  • Undone the same day. A wrong diagnosis moved emails ahead of texts in the 227 AT RISK workflows. All 227 were restored from backup and read back identical.
  • Still open. 37% of Lapsed runs stop at the first text, likely missing text permission. The fix is waiting on a decision.

01POS software optimization and management

Discount codes: consolidated, scaled, $84K a month kept.

The problem

Partner and promo codes were set up differently at every center. Discounts ran at 2.9% of sales.

What Fetch did

  • Audited 1,041 live codes across 80 businesses
  • One partner standard: 85 codes at 48 companies, one go-ahead
  • New customer, win-back and seasonal codes standardized portfolio-wide
  • Discount rate tracked monthly in Pulse

By hand

~85 hours of code entry, one company at a time.
