Vaccine & Room Sharing Requirements

Vaccines

  • Dogs (Boarding/Daycare): Rabies, Distemper, Bordetella, Influenza, and Negative Fecal (within 6 months)
  • Cats: Rabies & Feline Distemper
  • NO HANDWRITTEN VACCINE PAPERWORK

Spectrum requires Influenza and a Negative Fecal for boarding/daycare — stricter than Jamboree and the brand default. Always confirm this when booking dogs for Spectrum.

Room Sharing / Group Play

  • Must be neutered by 18 months to participate in group play
  • Dogs can only share overnight suites if they are siblings
  • An unspayed female and unneutered male may not be in the same group
