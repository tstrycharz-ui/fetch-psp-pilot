How it works

Fetch, the website chat assistant

Each location's website has its own assistant. It answers 24/7 using that location's own hours, services and prices.

  • It never books a stay. It collects the details and passes the chat to the location's team.
  • The location is notified the moment the chat needs a person.
  • On the main website, Fetch asks which location the customer wants and routes the chat there.

New-customer welcome

When a first-time customer sends an enquiry, they get a welcome email in their location's own design.

  • It carries the FETCH30 new-customer offer (the current promo code, which can be changed down the line) and the location's booking link.
  • Returning customers don't get it, and nobody gets it twice.
  • Each location keeps its own look and offer.
